# PROJECT CONTEXT — GatiPath

## Product Name & Identity
- **Product Name**: GatiPath
- **Tagline**: Learning that moves at your pace.
- **Target Audience**: Indian school students in Classes 6–10 (pilot focused on Class 7 Mathematics: Fractions), along with their school teachers.
- **Core Mission**: Replace one-size-fits-all classroom pacing with teacher-led, deterministic adaptive learning that detects prerequisite gaps and misconceptions without replacing the teacher as the primary decision-maker.

## Key Stakeholders
1. **Students (Ages 11–16)**:
   - Need clear, bite-sized lessons, visual explanations, and supportive feedback.
   - Often struggle when prior foundational concepts (like equivalent fractions or LCM) are weak.
   - Require clean, friendly, responsive UI optimized for low-end mobile devices and tablets.
2. **Teachers**:
   - Need actionable class diagnostics, not walls of vanity metrics or black-box predictions.
   - Require oversight to review learning gaps, inspect student attempts, and assign targeted interventions.
   - Retain full autonomy over student progression.
